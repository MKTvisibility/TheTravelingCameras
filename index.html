About The Traveling Cameras

The Traveling Cameras is a Colorado-focused photography marketplace and creative community within the MKT Colorado and MKT Visibility network. Our goal is to make local event photographs easy to discover, support photographers in communities across the state, and help businesses find talented local photographers.

Photographers will set individual digital image prices from $5 to $20. The planned marketplace fee is $2 per image plus applicable payment processing costs, with monthly photographer settlements.
